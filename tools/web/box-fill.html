Title 24 Part 3 / NEC Article 314.16 Minimum Volume Calculation & Line Breakdown

1. Conductor Fill — NEC 314.16(B)(1)

Count each conductor originating outside and terminating/spliced in box, plus through conductors.

Conductors originating and terminating entirely within box (pigtails) count as 0.
2. Clamp Fill — NEC 314.16(B)(2)
3. Support Fittings — NEC 314.16(B)(3)
1 allowance per stud/hickey based on largest conductor.
4. Device / Strap Fill — NEC 314.16(B)(4)

Each yoke/strap containing devices counts as double volume allowance (2x) based on largest conductor connected.

5. Grounding Conductor Fill — NEC 314.16(B)(5)

1 volume allowance based on largest EGC for up to 4 grounds. Add 0.25 allowance for each ground over 4.

Minimum Required Box Volume

16.00 cu in (262.19 cm³)
